TELECOM ATLAS
Original telecom component explorer. Demonstration only.

Geometry and inventory: original procedural files included under
public/assets/telecom_site.glb and telecom_inventory.json. No third-party models,
textures, HDRIs, or fonts are included. Geometry is unmodified; individual mesh
world translations and viewer materials change for conceptual inspection.
System fonts: Georgia, Segoe UI and Consolas, supplied by the operating system.

Three.js 0.180.0, MIT. See THREE.txt for the complete license.

Source reference: human-atlas by ashemag, MIT, commit
7a383d3ee2759e3ddf157c704fb8814fd0c50bcb
https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas/tree/7a383d3ee2759e3ddf157c704fb8814fd0c50bcb
Pointer gesture handling adapted from app/pointer-tap.ts. Measured packing
informed by app/explosion-layout.ts. See HUMAN-ATLAS.txt for the complete license.
No human-atlas identity, anatomy assets or data are included. Its BodyParts3D
and historical HuBMAP attribution does not describe the telecom geometry.

All displayed functions are illustrative, taken from the demonstration inventory
for equipment; descriptions for unassigned site context are viewer-authored.
Exploded arrangements are conceptual, not disassembly instructions.
